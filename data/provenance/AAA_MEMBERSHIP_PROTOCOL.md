# AAA Sovereign Membership Reconstruction Protocol

## Purpose

Construct the Shield component C_t of the Genuine Nation definition:

G_t = N_t union C_t.

## Baseline specifications

Two panels are maintained.

### Primary

aaa_primary_member_i(t) = 1 when entity i satisfies the preregistered primary AAA sovereign-rating criterion for year t.

### Extended

aaa_any_agency_member_i(t) = 1 when entity i satisfies the preregistered any-major-agency AAA criterion for year t.

The two specifications are never silently merged.

## Evidence requirement

Each membership interval must contain:

- entity;
- rating agency;
- rating grade;
- interval start year;
- interval end year;
- evidence_id;
- source edition or publication date;
- notes.

A present-day rating cannot be projected backward.

## Coverage limitation

Modern sovereign credit ratings do not provide continuous coverage to 1648. The observed AAA panel therefore begins only where defensible rating evidence exists.

Earlier historical Shield reconstruction, if attempted, is a separate capacity-proxy exercise and cannot be labelled AAA membership.

## Revisions

Rating changes are represented as dated intervals. Corrections or source revisions generate a new dataset version.
