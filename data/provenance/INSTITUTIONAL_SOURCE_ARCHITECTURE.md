# Institutional Source Architecture

## Primary providers

The FourNations empirical programme uses four institutional providers as the primary macro-financial data backbone:

1. International Monetary Fund (IMF)
2. World Bank (WB)
3. Bank for International Settlements (BIS)
4. Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

## Assignment by empirical workstream

### Historical and structural panel

Primary role: World Bank long-run development and macroeconomic indicators, supplemented by IMF and OECD where coverage is appropriate.

### Phase coherence

Primary role: harmonized macro-financial time series from IMF, BIS, OECD and World Bank. The exact phase transformation is versioned separately.

### Zero-IRP

Primary role: IMF and OECD macroeconomic expectations/price series, BIS financial statistics, with World Bank long-run macro controls.

### Labor and luxury

Primary role: OECD and World Bank labor, household, consumption and expenditure series, subject to explicit proxy definitions.

## Access rule

Every API request or downloaded artifact must be represented in the source register with provider, dataset/flow, series key or indicator, coverage, retrieval date, transformation, and output location.

No source is treated as interchangeable merely because it measures a similarly named variable.
