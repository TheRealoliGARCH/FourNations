# Nuclear Onset Evidence Catalog

This catalog separates the repository seed chronology from externally documented alternative conventions.

## Israel

### Baseline

The repository seed uses 1967 under an estimated-acquisition convention.

### Documented alternative: 1966

SIPRI Yearbook 2001 states that Israel has been a nuclear weapon power since late 1966, when its first bombs were manufactured. The `israel_late_1966` scenario therefore uses 1966 as a calendar-year onset under the `first bombs manufactured` convention.

This is not a claim that 1966 and 1967 are interchangeable measurements. They are distinct operationalizations and must remain scenario-labelled.

Evidence identifier: `SIPRI-YB-2001`.

## India

### Baseline

The repository seed uses 1974 under a first-nuclear-test convention.

### Documented alternative: 1998

SIPRI records India's 1974 first nuclear test and discusses the 1998 tests. The `india_overt_1998` scenario deliberately uses a different operational definition: public demonstration of nuclear weapon capability in 1998. This scenario is intended to test sensitivity to a threshold based on overt capability rather than first test.

Evidence identifier: `SIPRI-YB-1999`.

## Pakistan

The baseline uses 1998, corresponding to Pakistan's first nuclear tests. Current evidence gathered for this catalog did not establish a distinct alternative onset convention sufficiently documented for inclusion. No unsupported Pakistan alternative is added.

## Joint documented scenario

`joint_documented` combines the documented Israel 1966 and India 1998 alternatives while retaining Pakistan at its baseline 1998 first-test convention.
