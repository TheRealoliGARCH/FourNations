# SIPRI Source Protocol

## Role
SIPRI is the primary source for the empirical security and armament layer.

## Nuclear membership
The nuclear component of the Genuine Nation indicator is sourced from SIPRI's World Nuclear Forces research and SIPRI Yearbook tables.

For year t, nuclear_member = 1 when SIPRI classifies the entity as possessing a nuclear arsenal for the relevant observation date.

Source edition and observation date must be stored for each membership record.

## Military capability and burden
The SIPRI Military Expenditure Database is used for military expenditure in current and constant prices, share of GDP, and per-capita expenditure. These variables are robustness measures unless separately preregistered as membership criteria.

## Arms transfers
The SIPRI Arms Transfers Database may be used for robustness analyses of conventional military capability and external armament dependence.

## Coverage
The current Military Expenditure Database covers 1949–2025, with country coverage varying by series.
