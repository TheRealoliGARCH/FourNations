# Research Validation and Simulation Programme

## Objective

Operationalize the empirical validation, simulation testing, and policy roadmap of *A Unified Theory of Monetary-Political-Economic Equilibrium*.

The programme is falsification-first. Analytical claims are treated as hypotheses to be tested against historical data and computational experiments.

## Workstreams

### W1: Historical four-nation hypothesis

Construct a reproducible historical panel covering the paper's proposed 1648–2026 window.

Primary hypothesis:

- Systems with more than four relevant powers are associated with elevated probability of systemic crisis within five years.

Required outputs:

- explicit power-count rule;
- explicit crisis definition;
- chronology and provenance manifest;
- in-sample and out-of-sample evaluation;
- robustness checks under alternative power thresholds.

### W2: Phase coherence

Operationalize national phase angles and compute:

Q_t = |(1/N_t) sum_j exp(i theta_{j,t})|.

Test whether low coherence, including the paper's Q < 0.4 threshold, predicts subsequent crisis.

### W3: Zero-IRP

Estimate:

pi_i(t) = r_A(t) - r_f(t) - E_t[i(t)].

Test whether the inflation risk premium is statistically distinguishable from zero under explicitly defined estimation regimes.

### W4: Four-agent simulation

Represent each nation by monetary, reserve, phase, bilateral, labor, luxury, and utility state variables.

Required experiments:

1. baseline convergence;
2. stochastic shock stress tests;
3. fifth-agent entry;
4. one-constraint-at-a-time ablation;
5. comparative statics.

### W5: Policy translation

Maintain measurement, stress-monitoring, and intervention layers. No intervention mechanism is treated as policy-ready solely because it is theoretically specified.

## Falsification criteria

A claim is challenged if its preregistered prediction fails under the stated data definition, evaluation horizon, and robustness protocol.

## Execution order

1. Data dictionary and provenance manifest.
2. Historical N-versus-crisis backtest.
3. Phase extraction and coherence backtest.
4. Zero-IRP estimation.
5. Four-agent simulator.
6. Shock and ablation experiments.
7. Policy dashboard and conditional roadmap.
